## Notes

1. **Closing Stock re-valued.** Tally carries Closing Stock at **₹1,40,12,391** against Opening ₹1,44,57,824, so ₹4,45,433 of stock was consumed in July and flows through COGS as it should. An earlier draft of this statement was built when Tally showed Closing = Opening, which booked *zero* stock movement and flattered the month; that has been corrected at source. Effect of the re-valuation in isolation: COGS ₹23.81 L → **₹28.26 L**, gross margin 51.2% → **42.1%**, and CM2 turns negative. Gross margin is now a real gross margin.
2. **Employee cost corrected in Tally.** July payroll is a clean debit of **₹2,19,204**, and the Indirect Expenses group foots to ₹24,05,697.23 on that basis — matching the P&L screen exactly. An earlier draft of this statement carried an override here, taking a debit of ₹4,19,204 against a ₹4,45,091 credit; that entry has since been fixed at source and **the override is withdrawn**. Nothing in this document now departs from Tally except the Stock Transfer elimination. Worth noting for trend purposes: at ₹2.19 L, July payroll is ₹1.69 L below June's ₹3.88 L.
3. **STR removed both sides.** Tally's on-screen result is a Nett Loss of ₹3,51,328 *with* STR included. STR purchases (₹3,81,447) exceeded STR sales (₹3,79,756) by ₹1,691 — an internal transfer/valuation loss. Removing STR from both legs, per the June treatment, adds ₹1,691 back. This is the smaller of the two adjustments to the Tally screen; see the reconciliation bridge at the foot of this document.
4. **Media Buying Agency ₹1,80,000 kept in performance Marketing (CM2),** not in Brand Investment — it is a fee for running Google/Meta, not content creation. No Content Creation – Agency spend was booked in July, so the Brand Investment (CM3) line is nil, against ₹6.53 L in June.
5. **Quick Commerce is a net debit of ₹2,667.** Returns and credit notes exceeded gross Blinkit sales, while ₹8,224 of Blinkit channel fees were still incurred — the channel lost money outright. Small in absolute terms.
6. **Shiprocket cost is split across two Tally groups.** Freight Charges (Shiprocket) ₹1,52,561 sits directly under Channel Fees & Commissions, while Brand Boost / Delivery Boost / generic fees (₹8,377) sit under D2C Logistics — total Shiprocket ₹1,60,938. Consider re-grouping in Tally so all Shiprocket cost sits in one place.
7. **No Finance Costs, Cost of Fundraising, or Depreciation** booked in July. Treated as nil; confirm whether unbooked or recorded elsewhere.
8. **Scope.** Tally closing balances for 1-Jul-26 to 31-Jul-26. No adjustment layered on for GST, TDS, depreciation, interest or tax. Management P&L, not a statutory statement.
