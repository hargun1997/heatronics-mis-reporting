# D2C raise benchmarks — Series A, B, C (India first, global as a check)

Compiled 3 Nov 2026 from public sources (2024–2026 preferred). Purpose: default values for the
5-year plan's funding section (`docs/planning/funding-defaults.json`). ₹ in crore; $ at ≈ ₹84/$ unless the
source gave both. "Mid" = the value the plan uses; "range" = what the sources span. Nothing here is a
promise: a round is priced on the company, the lead and the year.

### How the mid values were chosen

**Series A.** Treelife (Jun 2026) puts a Series A at ₹20–80 Cr equity. Inc42's H1 2025 growth-stage (Series A/B)
median across all sectors was $8 M (≈ ₹67 Cr); D2C tickets run smaller (2024 D2C average $4.2 M across all stages,
Inc42). Named 2025 D2C Series As: Palmonas ₹55 Cr (Aug 2025), Moxie Beauty $15 M ≈ ₹125 Cr (Dec 2025, at > ₹100 Cr
ARR), Snitch ₹110 Cr (2023, at ≈ ₹240 Cr revenue). Those headline rounds came late and large; the "well-run but
ordinary" Series A sits lower. Mid ₹40 Cr on ₹40 Cr ARR. The ARR range is wide because the market moved: bootstrapping
to ₹5–10 Cr before a *targeted* Series A is now called "credible" (D2C Pulse, 2026), while the funded names above
raised their A at ₹100 Cr+ ARR. Consumer-VC blogs put ₹30–50 Cr as the omnichannel inflection and "₹50 Cr+" as where
investors look for proof of scaling without breaking unit economics (base.com, 2026).

**Series B.** Treelife: ₹50–200 Cr equity. Named: The Sleep Company ₹177 Cr (Nov 2022, > ₹200 Cr ARR), Foxtale
$30 M ≈ ₹250 Cr (Apr 2025), Snitch ≈ ₹280 Cr primary (Jun 2025, FY25 revenue ≈ ₹520 Cr), Heads Up For Tails ≈ $25 M
(2025), Ultrahuman $35 M (Mar 2024, heading to $100 M ARR). Mid ₹150 Cr on ₹150 Cr ARR; the big 2025 Bs were
₹250–340 Cr at ₹500 Cr+ revenue.

**Series C.** GIVA ₹530 Cr (Jun 2025, FY25 revenue ₹518 Cr), The Sleep Company ₹184 Cr (Dec 2023, ₹350 Cr net sales)
then ₹480 Cr half primary / half secondary (Aug 2025), Renee Cosmetics $30 M at a $200 M valuation (D2C Insider).
Mid ₹300 Cr on ₹350 Cr ARR. Round ≈ ARR at Series C in India (GIVA ≈ 1.0×, Sleep Co ≈ 0.5×).

**Multiples.** D2C Pulse (2026): 10–15× revenue at the 2022 peak, "2–4× revenue" in the 2024–26 playbook. eChai
(2026): 8–12× in the boom, 3–6× now; sub-$10 M-revenue brands are increasingly priced on EBITDA (8–18× in India).
Observed: Snitch Series A ≈ 2× revenue (Dec 2023, profitable fashion), Snitch Series B ≈ 4.5–5× FY25 revenue
(₹2,500 Cr valuation), GIVA Series C ≈ 7.5× FY25 revenue (jewellery, 90 % growth). US consumer Series A median
pre-money ≈ $45 M on $7–20 M revenue (Carta Q1 2025 via Eightx) ≈ 2–6×. Mid 3.5× / 4× / 4.5× with growth pushing
the top of each range. Sources disagree mainly on whether revenue or EBITDA is the base below ~₹100 Cr; the plan uses
ARR multiples because the engine reports ARR, but the EBITDA cross-check (8–18×) should be applied once EBITDA is
positive.

**Dilution.** Carta medians (US, via SaaStr / EquityList): seed ≈ 20 %, A ≈ 20 %, B ≈ 15 %, later 10–15 %; > 25–30 %
in one round is a warning sign. India: Entrackr (Sep 2025, TheKredible data) found ~11 early-stage startups diluting
> 40 % *cumulatively* by Series A, with founders left at 29–54 %. Implied from deals: Snitch A ≈ 22 % (₹110 Cr /
₹500 Cr), Snitch B ≈ 11 % (₹280 Cr primary / ₹2,500 Cr), GIVA C ≈ 13 % (₹530 Cr / ≈ $465 M). The mid values are
also what the mid round ÷ (mid multiple × mid ARR + round) gives, so the JSON is internally consistent.

**Gap between rounds.** India seed → A median ≈ 20 months (Seafund 2026, via Backrr); Carta (global, end-2024):
seed → A 2.2 y, A → B 2.5 y, B → C 2.4 y. Indian D2C examples run faster: Sleep Co B → C 13 mo, C → next 20 mo;
Snitch A → B 18 mo; Moxie seed → bridge 15 mo → A 17 mo. Mid ~20 months, plan for up to 30.

**Venture debt (India).** Treelife (Jun 2026): "usually 25 % to 30 % of the equity raise", ₹5–20 Cr beside a
₹20–80 Cr A, ₹15–50 Cr beside a ₹50–200 Cr B, ₹50 Cr+ at growth; tenure 18–36 months from drawdown; 13–15 % p.a.
(some 12–18 %); 3–6 month interest-only moratorium; warrants 0.1–2 % fully diluted at last-round price; prepayment
penalty 1–3 %; processing fee charged by some, no figure given; min-cash covenant 3–6 months of burn; eligibility
ARR ≥ ₹2–5 Cr. EquityList (citing Stride's 2024 report): 13–15 %, 18–36 months, warrants 0.1–2 %; tickets ₹2–200 Cr
(Trifecta ₹10–15 Cr typical, Alteria up to ₹150 Cr). D2C examples: Country Delight $8.3 M debt beside $20 M equity
(≈ 40 %), BlueStone $9 M venture debt + $12 M cash-flow financing (Inc42, 2024). Market: $1.3 B in 2025, 187 deals.

### Hardware / health devices vs FMCG & beauty

The sources do not publish a separate multiple for device brands; what they show:
- Device / wellness-hardware rounds in India come **smaller and later** than beauty / food: Curapod ₹20 Cr pre-Series A
  (Feb 2026), Aether MindTech ₹4.5 Cr pre-Series A (Aug 2025), Heatronics ₹1.8 Cr seed (Jul 2026), Frido undisclosed
  growth round at ≈ ₹7 Cr/month run-rate (Feb 2024). Beauty seeds in the same window were ₹14–28 Cr.
- Hardware needs inventory and capex, so **debt sits beside equity more often** (Dozee ₹58.5 Cr debt vs ₹6.3 Cr
  equity in Mar 2025; Country Delight / BlueStone above). Expect the venture-debt share at the top of the 25–30 % band.
- Device brands that reach scale **are valued like consumer brands** when margin is there: Ultrahuman went ₹107 Cr →
  ₹620 Cr revenue (CY23 → CY24) at an 8 % EBITDA margin and raised a $35 M Series B; The Sleep Company (mattress
  hardware, 60 stores) raised B → C at > 2× step-up in 13 months. Repeat / consumables (topicals, refills) move a
  device brand toward FMCG repeat metrics, which is what Series B diligence weighs most (Fireside / Sauce.vc via eChai).
- For Heatronics specifically (CDSCO Class B, ISO 13485, own factory): the medical-grade story supports GM and the
  offline / physio / B2B channels, but investors will still test the D2C checklist (repeat, CM2, CAC payback).

## 3. Caveats

- Most Indian deal reports omit valuation; multiples above are either stated ranges from advisory / investor blogs or
  back-solved from the few disclosed valuations (Snitch, GIVA). Treat 3.5× / 4× / 4.5× as planning defaults, not market.
- The named 2025 rounds are the survivors: D2C funding is 45 % below 2022 ($898 M in 2025 vs $1.6 B; 70 % of 2025
  value was seed / early, Tracxn via Entrepreneur India), so the median funded brand raised less, later, than these.
- "Series A" labels drift: Snitch's ₹110 Cr (2023) and Moxie's $15 M are Series-B-sized by 2020 standards; Curapod's
  ₹20 Cr is labelled pre-Series A. Use ARR-at-raise, not the label, when mapping Heatronics onto the table.
- Hardware / medical-device-specific multiples are not published; the device section is inference from a handful of deals.
- Venture debt processing fee: sources say "some charge a processing fee" without a figure; the JSON's 1.5 % is an
  assumption to be replaced with a term sheet. Interest accrues from drawdown (not after the moratorium).
- Dilution per round in India is often above the Carta medians (Entrackr, Sep 2025); the ESOP pool top-up before a
  Series A dilutes founders further and is not in the figures here.
- Exchange rate ≈ ₹84/$; sources printed between ₹83 and ₹88.
